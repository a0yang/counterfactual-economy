# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Counterfactual Economy is intended to be a constructive, cross-disciplinary project. Participants should be treated with respect regardless of professional background, nationality, academic school, programming experience or level of contribution.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique models, assumptions and evidence rather than people.
- Distinguish facts, empirical claims, model assumptions and opinions.
- Welcome good-faith disagreement.
- Credit sources and contributors.
- Avoid harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment or threats;
- personal abuse;
- deliberate misrepresentation of another contributor;
- plagiarism or presenting another person's work as your own;
- publishing private personal information without permission;
- sustained disruption of project discussions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, close or remove contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation where necessary to protect the community.

For sensitive concerns, contact the project maintainer privately rather than escalating a public discussion.
