# Project origin

This project began with a simple question:

> **If a government had made a different economic decision at a crucial moment in history, how different might the world have become?**

The initial concept comes from a non-programmer project founder who wants to connect economic history, economic policy, financial systems, data and simulation in one open platform.

The founder's role is to help maintain the project's vision, research questions and product direction while welcoming people with stronger expertise in software engineering, economics, quantitative modeling, data and game development.

This is intentionally an open invitation:

> **The idea is not finished. The community is expected to improve it.**
