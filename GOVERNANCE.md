# Governance

## Early-stage governance

The project is founder-initiated and community-oriented.

During the early stage, the founder maintains the project vision and coordinates major changes while encouraging contributors to challenge the design.

Technical and economic decisions should be documented in the repository rather than hidden in private discussion.

## Maintainers

As the project grows, maintainers may be added for areas such as:

- simulation core;
- economic models;
- data infrastructure;
- validation;
- interface / visualization;
- documentation and community.

## Model review

Major economic mechanisms should ideally receive review from at least one contributor familiar with the relevant domain.

Review is about clarity, empirical grounding, implementation quality and limitations. It is not a vote on which economic school is philosophically correct.

## Changing the architecture

Significant architecture changes should be proposed as issues or discussions and recorded in `docs/DECISIONS.md` once accepted.

## Future evolution

The governance model should evolve with the contributor base. A mature project may adopt a maintainer council, working groups or another transparent structure.
